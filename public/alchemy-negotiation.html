Where we're at

Boys — first of all, what you've built with us has been real. 203% ARR growth year-on-year, US$5.2M ARR, a sales engine that actually converts. None of that happens without the work you've done on creative, media buying, and funnel execution.

We're coming up on the raise close and scaling the business from ~$500K/month revenue toward US$2M/month by end of year. That means the commercial structure needs to evolve with us. What's below is two options. Both are uncapped, both scale directly with performance, both keep you as our primary marketing partner through the US expansion and the raise. The goal is to align what you earn with what the business actually makes — so every month we're both winning or both tightening the screws together.

How the commission base is calculated

Both options are calculated on contribution margin — the actual profit the business keeps after paying for ads, refunds, platform fees, and the sales team. This is the number we're both working to grow.

Pure performance-based tiered structure with no retainer: 8% on the first A$300K of contribution margin, 12% in the A$300-500K band, and 15% on every dollar above A$500K. Tier 3 matches Option A's flat rate, so at scale the two structures converge.

At target (A$150K ads) this pays A$18,405/mo (~US$11,595). Once we consistently clear A$500K of contribution monthly (stretch territory), the 15% top tier activates and earnings accelerate toward Option A's levels.

Why we think Option B is the right deal for both sides
For Alchemy
  • Tier 3 matches flat. 15% above A$500K contribution is the same as Option A's flat rate — once we're consistently at stretch territory, the two structures pay you identically at the margin.
  • Clean, no-strings structure. No retainer, no clawbacks, no minimums to defend. You get paid on contribution, period.
  • Scale = compounding upside. As we push toward US$2M/month revenue, tiers 2 and 3 activate, and your per-dollar earnings step up.
  • Uncapped forever. No ceiling. The better we do, the better you do.
For Collective Shift
  • Lower fee burden when MER is bad. When ads underperform, we're not paying premium rates on thin margins. Protects cash runway.
  • Aligned incentives. Both sides win when contribution grows. Both sides tighten when it doesn't.
  • Cleaner story for the raise. Investors want to see that marketing cost scales with outcomes, not ad budget.
  • Signals partnership maturity. Moving from "pay the agency" to "share the business" is what exit-ready companies do.

Proposed next steps

1. Pick your preferred option — or propose a hybrid you think is fairer. We've done the modelling but we're not married to every number. If the OPEX allocation should be 18% instead of 20%, or the tier 1 rate should be 9% instead of 8%, we can work it.

2. Backdate effective April 1. We both know the current structure wasn't going to last once we got past proving the model. Better to formalise the new one now than litigate which month it starts.

3. 12-month term with 90-day review. If either side thinks it's not working after Q1, we meet and adjust. No lock-ins, no games.

4. US trip — let's close it in person. I'm in Miami for the next stretch. Happy to fly to wherever you are or hop on a long-form Zoom once you've digested this. Aim to have terms agreed inside two weeks.
