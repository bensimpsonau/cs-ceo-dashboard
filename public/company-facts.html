Ready-to-Say Answers

"Where are you guys based?"
"Offices in New York and Melbourne. Ben splits his time between the US and Australia. Members in 40+ countries now, so it's not just an AU thing. Whereabouts are you?"
"How long have you been around?"
"Collective Shift is 6 years old. Ben's been in crypto personally for 9 years, started in 2017. Bootstrapped the whole way."
"Is Ben qualified? Who is he?"
"Ben Simpson founded CS 6 years ago. Educator, not a licensed adviser by design. 500k+ on YouTube at @BenCollectiveShift. Audited returns - 534% over 3 years, 68% last 12 months. Independent, no affiliate deals with exchanges. Coinbase and Swyftx use our research."
"What if CS goes under?"
"Fair question. We're bootstrapped, no outside investors, no debt. And the important bit - we never hold your money. You self-custody or use regulated exchanges in your own name. The frameworks, ship list, education stay with you whether CS exists tomorrow or not."
"How many members? Any Aussie-heavy?"
"1,100+ across 40+ countries. Started in AU but it's global now. Three membership tiers - Platinum, Pro, Legacy."
"Are you AFSL-licensed / regulated / can you give me advice?"
"We're educators, not licensed financial advisers. I can't tell you what to buy. I can show you the framework we use, the research, the exit rules - and you make the call. For licensed advice, we'd point you at an adviser in your jurisdiction."

Do and Don't

Do

  • Always say "offices in New York and Melbourne". Both, always.
  • Separate the two numbers: CS is 6 years old. Ben is 9 years in crypto.
  • Lead with the 40+ countries line when they ask about reach.
  • Use the audited returns numbers verbatim (534% / 68% / 116% CAGR).
  • Point to YouTube (@BenCollectiveShift) for proof of Ben's thinking.
  • If asked about competitors, politely decline to comment.

Don't

  • Don't say "since 2016" or "founded 2017". Those are wrong now.
  • Don't say "8 years in the space". Ben has 9.
  • Don't say "based in Australia, expanding to the US". That's outdated.
  • Don't say "members across Australia and the US". It's 40+ countries.
  • Don't volunteer biography in the opener. Answer when asked.
  • Don't give licensed-adviser style advice. We educate, we don't prescribe.
  • Don't name-drop affiliate partnerships. We have none, on purpose.
