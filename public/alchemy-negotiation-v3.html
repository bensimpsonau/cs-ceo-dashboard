Context

Boys, here's the refreshed compensation structure for the two of you. One rate. Clean rules. Real scale on the upside.

The structure in three lines:

  1. Flat 15% of contribution margin. No tiers, no cliffs, no step-ups to argue about.
  2. Three deductions before commission. Ad spend (the media that generated the sale), closer + setter commissions (15%), and a Platform Fee Allocation (20%, contract-defined). What's left is contribution margin. That's your base.
  3. MER Floor Protection. If monthly MER drops below 2.0x, commission is replaced by a flat A$10,000 retainer. Neither side subsidises a bad media month.

USD leads because that's where the growth is; AUD shown as secondary. FX: 1 USD = 1.587 AUD. Two planning scenarios shown throughout: 3x MER (conservative base) and 4x MER (stretch case).

MER Floor Protection

The one and only exception: below 2.0x MER

MER = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend. When monthly MER drops below 2.0x, each ad dollar returns less than $2 of revenue. After closer/setter commissions and platform costs, contribution goes negative. Paying commission at that point would mean paying to scale a loss.

Rule: MER < 2.0x in any month → no commission, A$10,000 flat retainer paid instead. Each month independent. MER recovers → commission structure resumes.
