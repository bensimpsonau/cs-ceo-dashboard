Boys — here's the refreshed framework. We've kept it simple and made the scale upside real. Everything below is built on a realistic planning base of 3x MER (every $1 of ad spend returns $3 in revenue), with clear triggers that protect both sides.

The proposal has three moving parts, designed to be honest, auditable, and aligned:

  1. Platform Fee Allocation — a contract-defined flat 15% of gross new-sales revenue, deducted before commission is calculated. Covers our platform OPEX (tech, team, tools) as a mechanical deduction. No audit rights needed — the number is what the contract says it is.
  2. Tiered commission — 12% up to A$500K/mo gross new sales, 15% at or above. The full amount flips to 15% when you cross the threshold, giving a real cliff-sized reward for scaling.
  3. MER Floor Protection — if monthly MER falls below 2.0x (meaning less than $2 returned per $1 spent), no commission is paid that month. Instead, Alchemy receives a flat A$10K retainer. Protects both sides from subsidising an underperforming media buy.

Commission Base = Gross Revenue − PFA. That's the pool commission is calculated on.

3

MER Floor Protection — How It Works

Why the 2.0x floor exists

MER measures how productive each $1 of ad spend is. At 2.0x MER, we're generating $2 of revenue per $1 spent — the threshold where the unit economics cover ad costs, merchant fees, commissions, and leave something for the business. Below that, every scaled $ is destroying value.

The rule: If monthly MER drops below 2.0x, no commission is paid. Alchemy receives A$10,000 flat for that month. Commission returns the following month once MER is back above floor. Each month stands on its own — no carry-forward, no penalty debt.
